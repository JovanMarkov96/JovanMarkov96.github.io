<h2 id="learn" class="q-h2"><span class="q-h2__ic"><i class="fas fa-graduation-cap"></i></span>Learn the theory</h2>

<p class="q-lead">Lecture notes, full video courses, and textbooks — roughly ordered from gentle introductions to graduate-level treatments. A good place to start is Watrous or de Wolf.</p>

<h3 id="lecture-notes-courses" class="q-h3"><i class="fas fa-chalkboard-user"></i> Lecture notes &amp; courses</h3>

- [John Watrous — Understanding Quantum Information and Computation (arXiv:2507.11536)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11536) — [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.11536) · [YouTube series](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOFEBzvs-VvqKKMXX4vbi4EB1uaErFMSO&si=OunACJUdcOOu6MTz) — a modern, self-contained course on the theory of quantum computing.
- [Ronald de Wolf — Quantum Computing: Lecture Notes (arXiv:1907.09415)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.09415) — Master's-level notes covering computation, the main algorithms, complexity, and error correction.
- [Artur Ekert — Introduction to Quantum Information Science](https://www.arturekert.org/iqis) — companion to the interactive [Qubit Guide](https://qubit.guide/index).
- [Scott Aaronson — Intro to Quantum Information Science (lecture notes)](https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=3943) — and the book-length [Quantum Computing since Democritus](https://www.scottaaronson.com/democritus/).
- [Andrew Childs — Lecture Notes on Quantum Algorithms](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~amchilds/qa/) — graduate notes on quantum walks, phase estimation, Hamiltonian simulation, HHL, and more.
- [John Preskill — Ph219/CS219 Lecture Notes](https://www.preskill.caltech.edu/ph219/) — the classic Caltech notes, with recorded lectures and chapters on error correction and topological QC.
- [John Watrous — The Theory of Quantum Information](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/TQI/) — the more mathematical, information-theoretic companion text.
- [Umesh Vazirani — Quantum Computing (Berkeley)](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~vazirani/quantum.html) — foundations of computation, algorithms, and complexity.
- [Ryan O'Donnell — Quantum Computation & Information (lectures)](https://www.youtube.com/@RyanODonnellTeaching/playlists) — full lecture playlists.
- [Nathan Wiebe — Quantum Computing Lectures](https://www.youtube.com/@nathanwiebe5305/videos) — video lecture series.
- [Joseph Avron — Quantum Information (Technion)](https://phsites.technion.ac.il/avron/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/07/Quantum_information_116031.pdf) — concise course notes (PDF).
- [MIT OCW — Quantum Information Science (MAS.865J)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/mas-865j-quantum-information-science-spring-2006/pages/lecture-notes/) — open courseware lecture notes.
- [Keio University — Understanding Quantum Computers](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/intro-to-quantum-computing) — beginner-friendly FutureLearn course.
- [CQuIC (UNM) — Courses](https://cquic.unm.edu/courses/index.html) — course materials from the Center for Quantum Information and Control.

<h3 id="books" class="q-h3"><i class="fas fa-book"></i> Books</h3>

- **Nielsen, Michael A., and Isaac L. Chuang** — *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information.* The standard reference.
- **Mermin, N. David** — *Quantum Computer Science: An Introduction.*
- **Kaye, P., R. Laflamme, and M. Mosca** — *An Introduction to Quantum Computing* (Oxford University Press, 2007).
- **Le Bellac, Michel** — *A Short Introduction to Quantum Information and Quantum Computation.*
